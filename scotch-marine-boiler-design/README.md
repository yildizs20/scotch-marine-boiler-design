# Scotch Marine Fire-Tube Steam Boiler Design

**3500 kg/h · 5 bar · natural gas · TS EN 12953**

Thermal and mechanical design of an industrial natural-gas Scotch marine fire-tube steam boiler, carried out as the term project for MAK 4040 – Steam Boilers at Istanbul Technical University (Spring 2026).

## Scope

- Combustion analysis and energy balance
- Furnace sizing and iterative flame-temperature modeling
- Convective heat-transfer surfaces and economizer design
- Draft and pressure-loss analysis
- Strength calculations according to TS EN 12953
- Benchmarking of all design parameters against commercial Turkish boiler manufacturers to check industrial feasibility

## Key Figures

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Steam capacity | 3500 kg/h |
| Pressure | 5 bar |
| Fuel | Natural gas |
| Indirect (heat-loss) efficiency | 90.68% |
| Design standard | TS EN 12953 |
